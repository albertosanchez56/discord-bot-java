Leona Support is ranked S Tier and has a 50.88% win rate in LoL Patch 25.15. We've analyzed 241741 Leona Support games to compile our statistical Leona Build Guide. For items, our build recommends: Plated Steelcaps, Locket of the Iron Solari, Celestial Opposition, Heartsteel, Knight's Vow, and Thornmail. For runes, the strongest choice is Resolve (Primary) with Aftershock (Keystone), and Inspiration (Secondary). The optimal Summoner Spells for this build are Flash and Ignite. Starting items should include Boots, Health Potion, and Oracle Lens. Leona's Best Support matchups include Mel, Heimerdinger, and Yuumi. Leona's Best Support counters are Teemo, Taric, and Soraka.
